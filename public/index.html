Three ways to build

Every app here uses the same avatars and the same API. What differs is how much of the conversation Atmee runs, and how much the app brings itself.

Full-stack

Most apps here

Atmee runs the whole conversation: it hears the user, thinks with the avatar's persona and knowledge, answers in the avatar's voice and shows its face.

  • ListenAtmee
  • ThinkAtmee
  • SpeakAtmee
  • FaceAtmee

When to use it

You want a talking character in your product without building a voice agent: a tutor, a host, an advisor, an interviewer. Your app steers it with what is on screen, events, its own tools and an agenda.

How it works

  1. Your server calls POST /v1/session with the avatar, a time limit and the tools it may use. The API key never reaches the browser.
  2. Your page joins the room it returns; the avatar arrives as live video and voice.
  3. Your page sends state and events; the avatar calls your tools and keeps to its agenda.

Speech-to-speech

Coming soon

One realtime voice model hears and answers in a single step, with the avatar's persona and voice, for the quickest back-and-forth.

  • ListenAtmee
  • ThinkAtmee
  • SpeakAtmee
  • FaceNext

When to use it

Voice-first products where speed matters most: phone-style assistants, quick questions, live interpreting. It is voice only for now; the face joins it next.

How it works

  1. Your server calls POST /v1/live/sessions and gets a WebSocket address and a token good for one minute.
  2. Your client connects with the token and streams the user's audio.
  3. The answers stream back as audio in the avatar's voice while the user talks.

Avatar-only

Two apps here

You bring the agent; Atmee brings the face. Whatever your app says, the avatar says it, lips in time, live in your own LiveKit room.

  • ListenYours
  • ThinkYours
  • SpeakYours
  • FaceAtmee

When to use it

You already have a voice agent (your own model, tools and text-to-speech), or audio from anywhere (a script, a recording, a live voice), and want a face on it. Waybill brings its whole agent; Facecast brings only audio.

How it works

  1. Your server mints a LiveKit token for the avatar in your own room, set to publish on behalf of your agent.
  2. It calls POST /v1/avatars/{id}/avatar_sessions with that token; the avatar joins your room.
  3. Your agent streams its speech to the avatar as 16 kHz audio, and the avatar publishes the face and the voice in sync. With LiveKit Agents, livekit-plugins-atmee does this part.
Add ?debug to the address of Ledger Academy, Northwind Talent, Kin, Isle of Whispers or Nightcap Live to see what it sends the avatar.
