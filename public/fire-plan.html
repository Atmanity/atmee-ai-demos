If there's a fire

1. Shout "Fire!" and get out, by your second way if the first is blocked. Stay low under smoke.
2. Go straight to the meeting spot. Never go back inside.
3. Call 911 from outside: "There's a fire at our address. Everyone is out" (or who is still inside).

Is there a fire right now?

Get everyone out now and call 911 from outside. Don't stop for belongings, and never go back in.
